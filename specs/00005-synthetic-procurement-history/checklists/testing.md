## Post-completion note — the E002 gate was discharged, 2026-07-26

This checklist was completed while FR-034 and SC-026 were gated on E002 publishing `manufacturer` and `part_number`. E002 published both, plus `manufacturer-catalog.json`. The items below are **left as answered**: they record what was asked of the artifacts at the time and were correctly satisfied then, and rewriting them would destroy the record of a gate having existed.

What changed underneath them: **CHK029** — FR-034 is no longer excluded from the epic's obligations, so the requirement completion denominator it asked for is now simply 37. **CHK037** — DV-021's exclusion check is still backed by FR-037, but it now runs over manufacturer names **drawn from E002's catalog** rather than a name space this epic invents, which is the case where the check earns its keep: a catalog we did not write is not self-evidently free of real firms.

Their intent survives the discharge. An item asking whether an exclusion was *published rather than presented as a clean sweep* was really asking whether the artifact tells the truth about its own coverage — which is the same question now that there is nothing to exclude, and is what `plan.md` § Compliance Result and T078 carry forward.
