# Feature Specification: Cross-Document Identity Resolution

## Problem Statement

The same physical material appears three times in a project's paper trail under three different names — a specification section calls for a manufacturer's switchgear by catalog family, a submittal names the manufacturer's full legal entity with a model number, and a purchase-order line abbreviates both and adds a unit convention neither of the others used. A coordinator asking "has the switchgear I specified actually been ordered, and when does it land" cannot answer it without joining those records by hand. All three legs are recoverable from the delivered corpus, and the third is recoverable in a different **shape** from the first two: submittal and purchase-order records carry the manufacturer and part number needed to match, and are **members** of the resolved identity. The specification leg links through the **`specification_section` a vendor printed on a submittal transmittal** — a direct reference E006 extracts, not a category inferred from a manufacturer's product range — and it is an **attribution the cluster carries**, not a third member. It has to be: E006 prints that field once per transmittal rather than against each line item, so every line item from a transmittal inherits it, and treating it as a co-granular member either drops legitimate records or produces a member with nothing to match on. The 26 real UFGS specification *documents* yield zero extracted values, so a specification document is never itself a candidate record. **The residual is therefore narrow and is published with its cause: a material whose transmittal printed no section number cannot be associated with its specification at all, and that absence is indistinguishable from a material that was never specified.** *(Rewritten at analysis remediation, A-4. This paragraph previously read "This feature links the two that can be linked and publishes the third as out of reach with its cause", which the fourth pass reversed when it restored the specification as a third cluster member on evidence from `0005_field_vocabulary.py` and `baseline.py`. SC-001 requires all three members present and US1's title names three, so the prose was not merely vague — it was wrong, and §Risks noted its survival without saying so.)* Nothing downstream — the risk-ranked worklist, the evaluation harness, the line-detail trace — can attribute a forecast to a specification requirement while that join is missing. The cost of getting it wrong is worse than the cost of not doing it: a wrong merge silently attributes one material's delivery history to another, and no surface in the system displays anything that would reveal it.

## Scope

### Included

- Normalization of manufacturer names to canonical identities through a versioned alias table, and of quantity units to a declared canonical form.
- Candidate-pair generation that reduces the comparison space without discarding true pairs.
- Pairwise scoring over string similarity and attribute agreement, with a two-threshold decision producing merge, withhold, or reject.
- Clustering of merged pairs into resolved entities under a constraint that prevents unscored records being joined transitively.
- A review-queue record for every withheld pair, shaped so a later review workspace can be built on it additively.
- Published quality evidence for the resolution run — merge precision, recall and blocking pair completeness with their intervals; coverage, reduction ratio and the withheld share as exact counts with their denominators, since a census over the run's own candidate set carries no sampling uncertainty.
- A deterministic **exact-match baseline** — normalized manufacturer plus part number, with no alias expansion and no fuzzy scoring — run over the same records and published beside merge precision and recall. It is the resolver minus the two components whose complexity is on trial, so beating it is the evidence that the alias table and the scorer earned their place, and it is plausibly winning on precision, which is the metric this feature optimizes.

### Excluded

- **The review workspace itself.** E016 owns the interface where a coordinator adjudicates withheld pairs. This feature emits the records and the contract they satisfy; it renders nothing. Excluded because building a surface for a queue whose shape is not yet proven would fix the shape prematurely.
- **The comparative evaluation harness.** E014 owns the frozen harness that runs identity resolution, retrieval and forecast calibration under one procedure, and the cross-capability comparisons that go with it. This feature runs its own baseline inside its own run (see Included) and does not build a harness. **Corrected during planning.** This bullet previously excluded *baseline comparison itself*, on the claim that "`specs/project-plan.md` scopes identity-resolution evaluation to E014, which owns … the baseline comparisons Principle VIII requires." **No such clause exists.** `project-plan.md:477` gives E014 a harness *covering* identity resolution, but the only baselines it registers are the forecast's naive and marginal arms (`:488`) and the retrieval ablation arms (`:487`). The registered PRD points the other way and wins under Governance: `prd.md:219` gates P1 release on every result being published "with its interval **and its baseline**", and `:156–157` register merge precision and recall as P1 metrics of CAP-004 — this feature's own capability. A citation nobody checked was excluding a Core Principle's requirement.
- **Learning from adjudications.** Nothing feeds a reviewer's decision back into the scorer. Excluded because a feedback loop needs an adjudication history that does not exist until E016 has run.
- **Resolution across projects.** Identity is resolved within a project, not across the five. Excluded because the same catalog number in two projects is two separate procurements, and merging them would corrupt both lead-time histories.
- **Manufacturer identity as a first-class registry.** The alias table maps strings to canonical names; it is not a vendor master with addresses, contacts, or corporate hierarchy. Excluded as outside CAP-004.
- **Blocking by embedding similarity.** Candidate generation uses deterministic keys only. Excluded because a vector-based block would make pair completeness depend on the embedding model and put a retrieval dependency in a path that does not need one.

### Edge Cases & Boundaries

- A submittal line item carries a manufacturer but no part number — it must still be blockable by manufacturer alias alone. (Specification sections are not candidates at all: E006 extracts no values from them.)
- A manufacturer appears under an alias absent from the table. The pair is not silently dropped; the unmatched alias is recorded so the table's gaps are visible rather than inferred from missing merges.
- Two records agree on manufacturer and part number but differ in a suffix encoding voltage, enclosure rating, or handing. These are different materials and must not merge — the case where normalization most plausibly destroys a distinction that mattered.
- A purchase-order line covers a material never specified, or a specification item is never ordered. Both are singleton clusters, not failures.
- Two records score above the merge threshold against a third but were never scored against each other. Under plain transitive closure they join anyway; the cluster constraint must prevent it.
- The labeled evaluation set contains a pair the blocking stage never generated. It is a blocking miss, not a scoring miss, and must be attributed to the stage that caused it.
- A record pair matches on both blocking keys and would be generated twice. It is one candidate pair, not two, or every published share has a double-counted denominator.
- A quantity unit is non-dimensional — each, lot, lump sum. It has no conversion to any other unit and comparing it dimensionally is meaningless.
- Every candidate pair falls in the withhold band. The run produces zero merges and a full review queue; this is a legitimate outcome, and precision over zero merges is undefined rather than zero.
- A pair scores exactly at a threshold. The boundary must belong to one band by definition, not by implementation accident.

## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - Link the same material across specification, submittal, and purchase order (Priority: P1)

A coordinator looking at a purchase-order line can see which submittal proposed that material and which specification section required it, because the records have been resolved to one identity despite naming it three different ways.

**Why this priority**: This is CAP-004's core value proposition and the join every downstream epic reads. E014 cannot evaluate resolution quality and E016 has nothing to review without it. Nothing else in this feature has meaning if the links are not produced.

**How the specification reaches the cluster — corrected three times, and the third correction is a design change rather than a citation fix.** E006 extracts no values from the 26 real UFGS specifications, so a specification *document* is not a candidate record. But it extracts **`specification_section`** from the 25 submittal transmittals, and **that field is document-scoped**: `baseline.py` places it in `DOCUMENT_SCOPED_TERMS`, so it is printed **once per transmittal at item ordinal 0** and never against a numbered line item, while `material_category` and `product_description` are per-item. **The specification is therefore a cluster *attribute*, not a cluster *member*.** Every submittal line item extracted from one transmittal inherits that transmittal's section, and a resolved entity's specification attribution is the distinct section its members carry. A cluster is two members plus an attribution — a submittal line item and a purchase-order line, attributed to the section the transmittal printed — not three co-granular members.

**Recorded as a design correction made at Analyze, with its cause named, so the fourth mis-specification of this leg is not attempted.** *(Owner decision on analysis finding A-6.)* The first three revisions all failed on the same unexamined assumption — that the three extracted fields are **co-granular** — and each fixed a different symptom of it: the first demoted the specification to a category-level association, the second withdrew even that on a false claim about the field vocabulary, and the third restored it as a **member** on correct evidence that the field is extracted while never checking at what *granularity*. The granularity is the defect, and neither reading available under the member model worked. If the value rides on each line item, then two submittal records for one material from two transmittals each carry their own section, so FR-041's cap fires **routinely** and its chosen disposition drops legitimate submittal records — the opposite of this story's purpose. If instead the section is its own ordinal-0 record, it carries neither manufacturer nor part number, FR-008's two keys generate no candidate pair for it, and SC-001's triple is unproducible without violating FR-018 and SC-005. The attribute model dissolves both: there is no unpaired ordinal-0 record, and no routine rejection.

**Independent Test**: Run resolution over a project's records and demonstrate that a known submittal line item and purchase-order line for one material appear as one resolved entity, each member traceable to its source record, and that the entity carries a **specification-section attribution** drawn from the extracted `specification_section` value the transmittal printed — so the coordinator can name the section the material was required by.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a submittal naming "Square D" and a purchase-order line naming "SQ D", **When** resolution runs, **Then** both resolve to one canonical manufacturer and the pair is scored rather than discarded.
2. **Given** two records whose part numbers differ only in a suffix encoding enclosure rating, **When** resolution runs, **Then** they are not merged, and the suffix difference is recorded as the attribute that separated them.
3. **Given** a submittal record carrying no part number and at least one purchase-order line sharing its manufacturer, **When** candidate generation runs, **Then** the submittal record enters a block on manufacturer alias alone and is scored against that purchase-order line.
4. **Given** a resolved entity, **When** its membership is inspected, **Then** every member record is traceable to its source document and location, and every pair the cluster implies carries a recorded score exceeding the merge threshold.
5. **Given** a submittal quantity in millimetres and a purchase-order quantity for the same material in inches, **When** the pair is scored, **Then** the unit difference contributes to the score as a scored attribute and does not exclude the pair from consideration.
6. **Given** two records whose quantities are expressed in non-dimensional units — "EA" and "LS" — **When** the pair is scored, **Then** the units are compared for equality only and are not converted into a shared canonical form.
7. **Given** a completed run, **When** any resolved entity is inspected, **Then** it carries at most one **distinct specification-section value**, drawn from the extracted `specification_section` its members inherited from their transmittals, and an attempt to admit a member whose section **differs** from the one the cluster already holds is refused naming the offending record — while a member whose section **agrees** is admitted without complaint however many transmittals covered the material. *(Third-pass residue: this scenario previously required the entity to assert **no** association to a specification section, contradicting SC-001, SC-041, FR-041 and US1's own narrative; the withdrawal it encoded was reversed at the fourth pass and this scenario was not carried with it. **Restated again at owner decision on analysis finding A-6**: the cap is over distinct section *values*, not over member rows, because the field is document-scoped and two transmittals covering one material is ordinary rather than a violation. The agreeing case is written into the scenario deliberately — it is the case the previous shape rejected.)*

### User Story 2 - Withhold uncertain pairs instead of merging them (Priority: P1)

A pair the system is not confident about is held back and written to a review queue, so a coordinator sees an open question rather than a silently wrong answer.

**Why this priority**: Principle III names this feature specifically — where an incorrect result would be invisible, the system must bias toward refusal. A merge-everything resolver would satisfy US1's mechanics while corrupting the record, and nothing in the interface would reveal it. This is security-critical in the sense that matters here: silent corruption.

**Independent Test**: Present a pair engineered to score inside the withhold band and demonstrate it produces a review-queue record and no merge, with the queue record carrying enough evidence for a human to decide.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a candidate pair scoring between the reject and merge thresholds, **When** the decision is made, **Then** no merge occurs, a review item is written, and the item carries both records, the score, the band it fell in, and the attribute agreements that produced it.
2. **Given** a run in which every candidate pair falls in the withhold band, **When** the run completes, **Then** it reports zero merges and a fully populated review queue as a successful outcome, and reports merge precision as undefined with its zero denominator rather than as a figure.
3. **Given** a withheld pair, **When** the resolved-entity output is inspected, **Then** the two records appear in separate clusters — withholding is not a deferred merge.
4. **Given** a normalization rule that would map two records known to be distinct onto the same key, **When** the collision guard runs, **Then** the run writes no resolved entities, no review items, and no manifest.

### User Story 3 - Read the resolution's own quality evidence (Priority: P1)

A technical evaluator can see how good the resolution is, with intervals on every estimated figure, and can tell which stage caused a miss.

**Why this priority**: `specs/prd.md` gates both identity metrics at "P1 release", so the MVP slice must carry the registered gate's evidence or the gate is unmeetable at the release it names. Raised from P2 at clarification for that reason. It also breaks a circular dependency: `specs/project-plan.md` gives E014 the LabeledPair entity while E014 depends on E009, so the frozen set has to be authored here and consumed there.

**Independent Test**: Run resolution against the frozen labeled set and demonstrate a report carrying merge precision, recall and blocking pair completeness with their intervals, and coverage, reduction ratio and the withheld set's size as exact counts with their denominators.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a completed resolution run, **When** the quality report is read, **Then** merge precision appears as a point estimate accompanied by a 95% interval, and the interval's method and sidedness are named.
2. **Given** a run with zero false merges among the merges made on labeled pairs, **When** the interval is computed, **Then** the rule of three is used with that merge count as its denominator, and the report discloses when that count falls below 30, where the approximation is not reliable.
3. **Given** a run with one or more false merges among labeled pairs, **When** the interval is computed, **Then** a one-sided exact binomial interval is used instead, because the rule of three does not apply once an error is observed.
4. **Given** a true pair the blocking stage never generated, **When** the report attributes the miss, **Then** it is counted against blocking pair completeness and against recall, and is not attributed to scoring.
5. **Given** a completed run, **When** any published figure is read, **Then** it carries either an interval or an explicit no-interval declaration with its denominator, per FR-038's classification, and a figure whose interval is undefined at the realized size is reported as undefined rather than bare.
6. **Given** a run whose merge precision or recall falls below its registered target, **When** the report is written, **Then** the shortfall is published with its cause rather than omitted or adjusted for.

### User Story 4 - Maintain the alias table as an auditable, versioned artifact (Priority: P3)

Someone maintaining the system can add a manufacturer alias, see which merges it would change, and know which alias-table version produced any historical resolution.

**Why this priority**: Future foundation. The MVP ships with a fixed table and never edits it, so nothing breaks without this. It earns its place because editing the table silently rewrites what past runs would have decided, and the version identifier that makes that visible costs nothing to record at the outset and cannot be back-filled later.

**Independent Test**: Change one alias, re-run resolution, and demonstrate that the run records a different alias-table version and that the affected merges are identifiable from the two runs' records.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an alias table in which one alias maps to two canonical manufacturers, **When** the table is loaded, **Then** loading fails with the offending alias named, rather than resolving the ambiguity at match time.
2. **Given** a completed resolution run, **When** any merge is inspected, **Then** it records the alias rule that fired and the alias-table version in force.
3. **Given** a manufacturer string matching no alias, **When** the run completes, **Then** the unmatched string is recorded, so the table's gaps are visible as data.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

**How to read a requirement in this section.** Roughly a third of the text below is *inline parenthetical revision history* — italicised notes recording that a clause was restated, what it said before, and why. **The bolded and unparenthesised text is the requirement; a parenthetical note is never an obligation.** The convention was adopted because five contradictions in this document survived multiple passes sitting immediately beside the clause they contradicted, and the reversal record is what let each of them be found. It has a cost the Spec Validator is right to flag: the notes make a requirement long, and a long requirement is one a reader skims. *(Recorded at analysis remediation, A-39. The finding's recommendation was that Clarifications and Stress-Test Findings own this content and the requirements be stripped of it. **That is not applied**, because the same remediation pass is required to strike-and-explain rather than delete superseded text (A-9, A-12, A-13, A-32, A-33), and moving a supersession note out of the clause it supersedes is what produced the failure mode in the first place — a reversal recorded in one place and left standing in another. #### Normalization

- **FR-001**: System MUST resolve every manufacturer string to a canonical manufacturer identity through an alias table, and MUST record which alias rule fired for each resolution.
- **FR-002**: The alias-to-canonical mapping MUST be a function. An alias resolving to two canonical manufacturers MUST fail table loading with the alias named, and MUST NOT be resolved by a runtime tie-break, a first-match rule, or a score.
- **FR-003**: Normalization MUST be additive: the raw string as it appeared in the source record MUST be retained alongside the normalized form, and no stage may discard the raw value.
- **FR-004**: Every merge MUST reference the alias-table version in force when it was decided. *(Reduced at analysis remediation, A-15. The first clause — "System MUST record an alias-table version identifier on every resolution run" — is stated by FR-034, which enumerates the manifest's contents, and again by FR-040, which requires the catalogue digest recorded "alongside the alias-table version". Three statements of one obligation are three places for it to drift, and they had already drifted on hash plurality. **The manifest's record of the version is FR-034's; the per-merge reference is this requirement's**, and it is a distinct obligation because a merge is a row and the manifest is another.)*
- **FR-005**: System MUST canonicalize dimensional quantity units to a declared base-unit form, and MUST classify non-dimensional procurement units — each, lot, lump sum — as arbitrary, comparing them only for equality after alias mapping and never converting them. **The mapping from an observed unit spelling to its class, its base unit and its conversion factor MUST be a versioned unit alias table carried by the same version identifier as the manufacturer alias table** (FR-004), so a change to a unit rule is a version bump exactly as a change to a manufacturer alias is, and "which rules produced this quantity" has one answer rather than two. **A dimensional unit MUST carry both a base unit and a factor to it; an arbitrary unit MUST carry neither**, so "never converted" has nothing to multiply by rather than resting on a code path declining to multiply. **The declared base-unit form MUST be recorded on the alias table's own header**, not chosen per run. *(Specified at analysis remediation, A-30. The requirement invoked "alias mapping" for units while no requirement and no Key Entity defined a unit alias table, and the base-unit half was ungraded — SC-009 grades only the arbitrary-unit half. `data-model.md`'s `unit_alias` and `manufacturer_alias_table.base_unit_system` already implement exactly this, and ambiguity A-9 recorded the single-versioning reading as an inference rather than as a requirement; it is now the requirement.)*
- **FR-006**: Unit agreement MUST contribute to a pair's score as a scored attribute and MUST NOT act as a hard filter that excludes a pair from consideration.
- **FR-007**: System MUST verify that no normalization rule maps two records known to be distinct onto the same key, tested over the labeled negative pairs, and MUST fail rather than emit a resolution when the guard is breached.

#### Candidate generation

- **FR-008**: System MUST generate candidate pairs such that a record missing either a canonical manufacturer or a part number still enters comparison, using manufacturer and part-number prefix as independent keys rather than requiring both. **The prefix length MUST be a committed constant declared beside the threshold and weight constants (FR-016, FR-047) and MUST NOT be supplied at runtime**, because it fixes the size of the candidate set and therefore fixes two published figures — the reduction ratio and blocking pair completeness — exactly as the cutoffs fix coverage. A prefix length changed after reading a reduction ratio is the same post-hoc tuning FR-016 forbids one stage earlier. **The committed length MUST be stated wherever those two figures are published** (FR-010, FR-011), so a reader can tell a genuine blocking improvement from a widened key. *(Specified at analysis remediation, A-30: the requirement named "part-number prefix" as a blocking key and never said how long a prefix is, though the length determines two figures the run publishes and, unlike the thresholds and the weights, it was not a committed constant.)*
- **FR-009**: A candidate pair MUST be identified by its unordered record pair. A record pair matching on more than one blocking key is one candidate pair, and MUST be counted once in every published denominator.
- **FR-010**: System MUST measure blocking pair completeness against ground truth sampled independently of the blocking keys, and MUST state the sampling frame with the figure. **The frame statement's content is specified rather than left to the renderer, and MUST carry**: *(a)* the population sampled from — the within-project pair space, named as such; *(b)* the assertion that the sampling method is independent of the blocking keys, together with the committed prefix length of FR-008, since a frame that does not name the keys it is independent *of* cannot be checked; *(c)* the stratum's realized size and its true-pair count; and *(d)* the stratum's identity — which of FR-037's two strata, and the hash it was frozen into. **A frame naming only "sampled independently" is not a frame statement**: it restates the requirement and lets an implementation that sampled from the candidate set satisfy the wording. *(Content specified at analysis remediation, A-30, in the same form FR-013 and FR-032 were given at requirements-quality review; this requirement said "state the sampling frame" and specified nothing about the statement, in a spec that had specified exactly that twice elsewhere. Every element lands on an existing column — `labeled_pair_set.sampling_frame`, `.pair_count`, `.true_pair_count`, `.stratum`, `.set_hash` — so nothing here asks for a schema change.)*
- **FR-011**: System MUST publish blocking pair completeness and reduction ratio as figures separate from merge precision, and MUST NOT fold blocking losses into a scoring metric. **"Scoring metric" means a figure whose denominator is the set of pairs the scorer decided** — merge precision and coverage. **Recall is deliberately not one**: FR-030 counts a true pair that blocking never generated as a recall miss, so recall spans both stages by design, and this prohibition does not reach it. The two are reconciled by attribution rather than by separation: FR-030's miss is *attributed* to blocking and appears in blocking pair completeness as well, which is what SC-024 certifies. **What the prohibition forbids is the opposite move** — reporting a blocking loss as a scoring outcome, so that improving the blocker looks like improving the scorer. *(Defined at analysis remediation, A-30: "scoring metric" was undefined and read against FR-030's explicit treatment of never-blocked pairs, so the two requirements appeared to contradict each other on whether a blocking loss may enter a published figure.)*
- **FR-012**: System MUST record every candidate pair it generated, so a true pair absent from the candidate set is attributable to blocking rather than inferred from a missing merge.
- **FR-013**: System MUST record every manufacturer string that matched no alias, so gaps in the alias table are visible as data rather than deduced from absent merges. **Each record MUST carry**: the raw string as the source printed it, its normalized form, the source record and the run it was observed on, the project, and the alias-table version whose gap it is — so a gap is groupable across records by its normalized form and attributable to the version that had it, which is what makes "adding this alias closes this row" a checkable claim. A stored occurrence count MUST NOT be kept: two records printing one unmatched spelling are two pieces of evidence, collapsing them would understate the gap, and the count is a query over the rows rather than a second answer beside them. *(Content specified at requirements-quality review: the requirement said only that the strings are "recorded", which is satisfied by a bare list that cannot be attributed to a run, a record, or an alias-table version — and therefore cannot show whether a gap was closed or merely stopped being observed.)*

#### Scoring and decision

- **FR-014**: System MUST score each candidate pair on the agreement of its attributes, and MUST record each component's contribution to the total. **The scored attribute set is exactly four and is enumerated here normatively: manufacturer, part number, product description, and quantity unit.** Every candidate pair MUST carry one recorded component per attribute — including an explicit *absent* component contributing zero where the attribute is missing on one side, so a missing value is visible as absence rather than as agreement or as disagreement. **No fifth attribute may be scored and none of the four may be omitted**, because FR-047 freezes exactly four attribute weights and SC-043 certifies four independent refusals: a fifth scored attribute would carry an unfrozen weight, and a fourth omitted one would leave a frozen weight unexercised, and neither is detectable from the weight vector alone. *(Stated normatively at analysis remediation, A-30. The set of four appeared only in the Glossary's "Attribute agreement" entry, which is not a requirement, while FR-047 and SC-043 both depend on the cardinality being exactly four. The Glossary entry stands as the definition; this clause is the obligation.)*
- **FR-015**: System MUST apply two thresholds producing three disjoint outcomes — merge, withhold, reject — and MUST assign each threshold's exact value to exactly one band, so no score is undecided or doubly decided.
- **FR-016**: Both thresholds MUST be calibrated against the frozen labeled set before any resolution run is published, MUST be recorded as committed constants, and MUST NOT be changed after observing a run's precision or recall.
- **FR-017**: System MUST freeze the labeled evaluation set and hash **each of FR-037's two strata separately** before threshold calibration, and MUST verify **both** stratum hashes on every run that reports against the set. Where this specification writes "the labeled-set hash" it means both stratum hashes; verifying one is not verifying the set. *(Made plural at requirements-quality review: FR-037 already mandated separate hashes per stratum, while this requirement, FR-034, FR-044, FR-047, SC-027, SC-040 and SC-043 all referred to a single hash, under which verifying the estimation stratum alone would satisfy the wording. **Correction, analysis remediation, A-34: that annotation over-reported what was edited.** Only this requirement, FR-034 and SC-040 were actually made plural; **FR-044, FR-047, SC-027 and SC-043 kept the singular** and were corrected only now — as did Key Entities' ResolutionRun row, which the annotation did not list at all. The blanket rule in the sentence above was what made the four correct-by-definition, and correct-by-definition is exactly the state in which a stale wording survives a pass that believed it had been edited.)*

#### Clustering

- **FR-018**: Every pair induced by a resolved entity's membership MUST carry a recorded score exceeding the merge threshold. A pair joined into a cluster without having been scored is a defect, not a weaker merge.
- **FR-019**: System MUST measure merge precision over the **estimation stratum's** labeled pairs that the emitted clusters induce, MUST NOT compute it across the union with the hard-negative stratum, and MUST state that population with the figure.
- **FR-020**: System MUST resolve identity within a project and MUST NOT merge records across projects, even where manufacturer and part number agree exactly.

#### Withholding and review

- **FR-021**: A withheld pair MUST NOT be merged by any subsequent stage, including clustering, and MUST NOT be merged transitively through a third record — withholding is a terminal decision within the run, not a deferred merge. **Review-item cardinality is not this requirement's**: FR-043 owns it, and states it once, per run. *(Reduced at analysis remediation, A-14. This requirement and FR-043 both stated "exactly one review item for every withheld candidate pair" — FR-021 scoped "within a single run" and FR-043 "per run" — which is one obligation written twice. STF-004 **restated** it here rather than replacing it, and the criteria had already split the way the requirements now do: SC-011 grades the within-run case and SC-039 the cross-run case. The no-merge clause is the half only this requirement carries.)*
- **FR-022**: A review item MUST carry both source records, the pair's score, the band it fell in, the per-attribute agreements that produced the score, and the alias-table version in force.
- **FR-023**: The review-item record MUST be shaped so that a later workspace can add adjudication state without altering the fields this feature writes, and **the shape is named rather than left as an intent**. The review item MUST carry: **no adjudication column** of any kind — no verdict, no reviewer, no decided-at, no note; **no reserved status value** and no status column at all; and **no `pending` member** in any enumeration on the record, since a `pending` state is an adjudication column wearing a different name and its absence is what makes E016's addition additive rather than a migration of this feature's rows. **The join surface E016 keys its own adjudication table on MUST be the stable unordered-pair identity of FR-009 together with the run identifier of FR-043** — pair identity, not a surrogate row id — so an adjudication anchors to the pair and survives a later run re-withholding it. *(Shape named at analysis remediation, A-16, in the same form FR-013, FR-025, FR-031, FR-032 and FR-049 were each amended: the requirement stated an intent with no statable satisfaction test, which is why the Requirement ↔ Criterion Map recorded it as a known hole and STF-008 left it open. This is what `data-model.md` §`review_queue_item` already builds and what task T050 already asserts; the requirement now says it, so the assertion grades a requirement rather than a design choice. Graded by SC-044.)*
- **FR-024**: A run in which every candidate pair is withheld MUST complete and report zero merges with a populated review queue, and MUST NOT be reported as a failure.

#### Publication and provenance

- **FR-025**: Every published figure MUST carry either an interval or an explicit no-interval declaration with its denominator, per the classification in FR-038. A figure whose interval is undefined at the realized sample size MUST be reported as undefined together with its denominator, never as a bare point estimate. **The no-interval declaration MUST state the reason it declines one, drawn from a closed set of exactly two licensed reasons** rather than being free text, so a census cannot be rendered indistinguishable from an estimate whose interval was quietly dropped. ### Risks

- **The published precision criterion may be unreachable as written, and this branch may not correct it (likelihood: high, impact: high).** `specs/prd.md` and `specs/sad.md` both require merge precision "≥ 0.95 on 40 hand-labeled pairs, published with its rule-of-three error bound". Read as the *bound* clearing 0.95, this is arithmetically impossible at this sample size: the rule of three gives a 95% error-rate bound of 3/n, so even at zero errors with all 40 pairs merged the lower bound on precision is 0.925. A 0.05 bound needs at least 60 merges; on a balanced labeled set the realistic denominator is nearer 15–20, giving 0.85 or wider. A correct implementation would fail it.

  **Amendment need — recorded here, not performed.** Both documents are registered, and `project-instructions.md` § Governance reserves amendments to the default branch: a feature branch records the need and does not perform it, and may not route it to another feature branch. **Two distinct needs, both owed:** *(a)* state whether 0.95 is the point estimate or the interval's lower bound, and if the latter, reconcile it with the labeled-set size — this specification assumes the former and says so in Assumptions; *(b)* the registered criterion names only the rule of three, which is inapplicable once a single false merge is observed, so an estimator for the non-zero-error case must be named. FR-027 adopts a one-sided exact binomial interval for that branch, which is a divergence from both registered documents and is recorded here rather than silently taken. **Both needs name `specs/prd.md` and `specs/sad.md`; neither is performed on this branch.**

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Blocking** | Restricting comparison to record pairs sharing a key, so the comparison space is reduced without scoring every possible pair. |
| **Candidate pair** | An unordered pair of records sharing at least one blocking key, counted once however many keys generated it. |
| **Pair completeness** | The share of true pairs that survive blocking into a shared block. A pair lost here cannot be recovered by any later stage. |
| **Reduction ratio** | The share of all possible pairs that blocking eliminates from consideration. |
| **Withhold band** | The score range between the reject and merge thresholds, in which a pair is neither merged nor rejected but routed to review. |
| **Coverage** | The share of candidate pairs the system decided automatically, rather than withholding. |
| **True pair** | Two records an annotator judged to describe the same material, recorded in the frozen labeled set. Established by judgment over an independently sampled frame, never derived from a blocking key. |
| **Attribute agreement** | The per-attribute comparison outcome — manufacturer, part number, description, quantity unit — contributing a recorded component to a pair's score. |
| **Recall** | The share of true pairs in the frozen labeled set that were auto-merged. A true pair withheld, rejected, or never generated by blocking counts as a miss. |
| **Census figure** | A figure computed over the whole of the run's own candidate set — coverage, reduction ratio, withheld share — carrying no sampling uncertainty and therefore no interval. |
| **Specification-section attribution** | The single distinct `specification_section` value a resolved entity's submittal members inherited from their transmittals, exposed at cluster level. An **attribution, not a member**: E006 prints the field once per transmittal rather than per line item, so every line item from one transmittal carries the same value and a cluster covered by several agreeing transmittals has one attribution and many members. |
| **Induced pair set** | Every pair implied by a cluster's membership, including pairs whose records were never directly compared. |
| **Rule of three** | An approximation giving a 95% upper bound of 3/n on an error rate when zero errors are observed in n trials. Applies only at zero errors, and is unreliable below n = 30. |
| **Arbitrary unit** | A unit with no defined relation to any other — each, lot, lump sum — which cannot be converted or dimensionally compared. |
